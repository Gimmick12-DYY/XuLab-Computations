## Amendment 6 (2026-11-23, which distal links carry signal; distal links are kept, per the 2026-11-09 ruling; written before any number)
m57_distal.py, observed only (no shuffle), 63 TFs, top 10,000 peaks, targets top 5 % / 1 % of |score| per TF (either / up / down). Pure distal links = TF-gene links through a non-self ABC element with no summit in the gene's promoter element and no summit within 10 kb of its TSS. Per TF-gene link: best ABC.Score among bound linking elements; strongest linking peak signalValue; number of distinct bound linking elements; loop support; H3K27ac; element class (of the best-ABC element).
A ABC bins 0.02-0.03 / 0.03-0.05 / 0.05-0.1 / 0.1-0.2 / > 0.2. B within-TF quintiles of the strongest linking peak signalValue; top 100 / 250 / 500 distal genes per TF by that value. C Peakachu loops (hic/work_wt293t/loops/peakachu_5000.loops.0.7.bedpe, 5 kb anchors): an element-gene pair is loop-supported if the element midpoint lies within one anchor +- 5 kb and the TSS within the other anchor +- 5 kb (either orientation); loop-only rule without ABC: gene linked if a summit lies within an anchor +- 5 kb and the TSS within the partner anchor +- 5 kb (all such links, and the subset with no promoter / 10 kb link). D bound elements per link 1 / 2 / 3-4 / >= 5. E element in the top half of candidate H3K27ac (EnhancerList H3K27ac.RPM.quantile >= 0.5) vs not; ChromHMM skipped (ENCFF071AXS file not available on Longleaf). Class: ABC class of the best-ABC element (genic / intergenic; non-self links carry no promoter class in this build). F nearest-gene rule: each summit > 10 kb from every universe TSS assigned to the nearest universe TSS (any distance; also capped at 100 kb), all such links and the subset with no promoter / 10 kb link. G ABC > 0.05 and top peak quintile; loop-supported and top peak quintile. Main rows appended as block "distal links" to stage1_precision_recall_report.xlsx.
