# Per-cell on-target signal (spec, 2026-11-11)
Label on every table: "diagnostic, no Perturb-seq". Question: is the low on-target share of each TF's reads (< 2% at its own sites; m10 Amendment 2 G1) uniform over cells, or do a subset of cells carry the TF-specific reads while the rest look like generic open-chromatin signal? TFs: NRF1, CTCF, ZNF143, NFYA. Read only under /vast/som/xujie_lab.
Inputs: per-TF fragment BED (/vast/som/xujie_lab/TAGATG-293T/bigwig/fragment/TF.<TF>.bed.gz; column 4 = library:cell barcode; 5' end = + start / - end - 1); cells and libraries from combined/Cell1000/Final_DNA_cells_TF1000cells_qc20_ForSubset.csv (DNA_id = sub-library); union ATAC (XuLab/data/HEK293T_ATAC_union3.bed.gz); own-motif sites in open chromatin and their ATAC-decile-matched background positions (m10 Amendment 2 site lists, work/g/lists.npz); for CTCF also ENCODE HEK293 ChIP summits in open chromatin (same file, labelled HEK293); HOT bins (m11 recall/hot_bins.bed); lab q <= 0.05 peaks.
1. Per cell: total fragments; fragments in union ATAC; fragments with a 5' end within +/- 100 bp of an own-motif site (union of windows); the same at the background positions; in HOT bins; in the TF's q005 peaks (FRiP-like). Per-cell enrichment = (own-site fragments / own sites) / (background fragments / background positions).
2. Overdispersion: expected own-site count per cell = fragments in union ATAC x pooled own-site rate of the TF; Pearson dispersion phi = sum((o - e)^2 / e) / (n_cells - 1), against 1,000 binomial simulations with the same per-cell n and pooled rate (seed 20261111); the same statistic for the cells of a swapped TF scored on this TF's sites (pairs NRF1-ZNF143, CTCF-NFYA).
3. Held-out cell ranking: sites and background positions split by chromosome (odd autosomes = selection, even autosomes + chrX = measurement). Cells ranked by selection-half own-site fragments per union-ATAC fragment; for the top 10/25/50% vs the rest, enrichment on the measurement half (pooled over cells), with 1,000-draw bootstrap over cells; the same for the swapped-TF cells.
4. Per-cell enrichment against fragments per cell (Spearman) and by sub-library (DNA_id) and dataset prefix: pooled enrichment per sub-library with bootstrap interval over cells. Descriptive; no cause is named that was not tested.
