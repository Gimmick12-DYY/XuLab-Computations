# m53: within-TF percentile of the Perturb-seq per-gene score for Stage 1 (spec, 2026-11-23, written before any number)
User: a fixed score cutoff is confounded by depth (cells per TF); use a "percentile of gene" score per TF, and draw histograms of each TF's target genes over it.
Input: the m51 paper per-gene score (m51 Amendment 2 mapping), recomputed at full precision with the m51_paper_score.py code (the saved tsv has 4 decimals; agreement checked to <= 5e-5); 63 TFs x 11,228 genes, own gene removed (Stage 1 OK mask). Candidate links: top 10,000 lab peaks per TF, promoter / ABC-linked element / within padding (m48 lists; 10 kb main, 0 and 50 kb).
Percentile: per TF over its OK genes, average ranks (ties averaged); |score| percentile = 100 (rank - 0.5) / n (100 = largest |score|); signed percentile the same on the signed score (0 = most down, 100 = most up). Top X % = percentile > 100 - X; bottom X % = percentile < X.
Part 1: per TF SD of the score and the 90th / 95th / 99th percentile of |score| vs top-25% cells per TF (Spearman); scatter (cells, 95th percentile of |score|).
Part 2: pooled histogram of the targets over the within-TF percentile (20 bins of 5), share of targets per bin pooled over TFs, with the matched gene shuffle (targets redrawn within NTC expression decile x promoter ATAC decile strata per TF, multivariate hypergeometric over bins, 1,000 shuffles, seed 20261118): mean and 2.5-97.5 %. |score| and signed versions; 63 / 55 non-broad (m45 grouping) / without NRF1, E4F1, DNMT1 at 10 kb; 63 TFs at 0 and 50 kb. Per TF: 10 bins with its own band (pages sorted by cells per TF) and a table: targets, share in the top 10 / 5 / 1 % of |score|, signed top 5 % (up) and bottom 5 % (down), shuffle mean, ratio, one-sided p (1,000 shuffles, seed 20261118).
Part 3: Stage 1 (m50 stage1 code) with responders = top 10 / 5 / 2 / 1 % of |score|, and signed top X % (up) / bottom X % (down); recall, shuffle mean, gap, observed / shuffle, p, precision observed / shuffle; 10 kb main, 0 and 50 kb; 63 / 55 non-broad / without NRF1, E4F1, DNMT1; leave-one-TF-out at 10 kb; one shuffle run (seed 20261118). Per TF observed / expected in-both ratio (exact hypergeometric mean) at 10 kb for top 5 % of |score| and, for comparison, |score| >= 0.3, each vs cells per TF (Spearman).
Figures: palette Option C, Arial, _slide (12.9 x 5.9 in, labels >= 12 pt) and _paper (183 mm wide), PNG / PDF / SVG, no explanatory text on panels. Outputs new only (regulatome/m53_stage1_percentile). Descriptive.
