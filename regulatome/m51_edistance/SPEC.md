User: "what about just use the e-distance Perturb-seq paper used?" In X-Atlas/Orion, E-distance is one number per perturbation (20 PCs, perturbed vs NTC cells); the per-gene quantity there is z-normalised expression (x - mean_NTC) / SD_NTC.
Cells: m23 top-25% sgRNA cells (cell_selection top25_own) of the kept units; NTC = pass_guide_filter Non-Targeting cells. Re-streamed from the m23 inputs (m51_stream.py): per GEM, NTC mean and variance of log1p(CP10k) over all NTC cells; a random sample of up to 100 NTC cells per GEM (seed 20261120) and all top-25% knockdown cells, log1p(CP10k).
PART A, E-distance. Genes: the 11,228-gene Stage 1 universe (main) and the top 2,000 highly variable genes (normalised dispersion of NTC CP10k within 20 mean bins; second column), own target gene(s) removed. Per gene z-scaling and 20-component PCA (randomized SVD, seed 20261120) fitted per TF on 5,000 random sampled NTC cells plus all top-25% knockdown cells of the panel. Per unit (TF level: constructs pooled, HP1 = CBX1/3/5 pooled; constructs also separately): NTC reference = NTC cells of the knockdown cells' GEMs, r_g = min(round(1000 k_g / n), available_g - k_g) per GEM (k_g knockdown cells in GEM g). E = 2 mean d(KD, NTC) - mean d(KD, KD) - mean d(NTC, NTC), Euclidean in 20 PCs, within-group means without self pairs. Null: 1,000 draws of k_g NTC cells per GEM from the reference, scored against the rest of the reference; p = (1 + #null >= E)/1001; BH q over the 63 TFs. Report cells, on-target knockdown % (100 (1 - KD/NTC mean CP10k of the own gene)), E, null mean and 95th percentile, E / null mean, p, q. Spearman of E / null mean with cells, on-target knockdown % and the number of |z| >= 3 responders per TF.
PART B, Stage 1 by E-distance group with the existing |z| >= 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 responders: top 10,000 lab peaks, 10 kb (main), 0 and 50 kb; TF groups: all 63, q < 0.05, q >= 0.05, top / middle / bottom third by E / null mean (11,228-gene version), each also without NRF1 / E4F1 / DNMT1; recall, shuffle mean, gap, observed / shuffle, p, precision observed / shuffle, TFs and pairs; matched gene shuffle as m50 (1,000, seed 20261118, one run for the table, groups share per-TF draws). Per TF: Spearman of E / null mean with the TF's observed / expected in-both ratio at |z| >= 2 and >= 3 (expected = exact hypergeometric mean over strata).
