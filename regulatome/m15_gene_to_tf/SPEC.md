# Gene-to-TF direction, rank-based, confident pairs only (spec, 2026-11-11)
Label: "diagnostic / development, benchmark already read" (the knockdown matrix has been compared with binding per TF many times). The per-gene direction itself (for a gene, which knocked-down TFs move it, against which TFs bind it) has not been read: the repo journal has no such entry; m11_compare.py contains it but was never run; m4 B6 (b6_core diagonal) is per TF list, not per gene.
Ask (Yuyang, relayed verbatim): "Gene to TF direction seems to be nice, but as you said the each TF is poor, could we do a ranked-base and only keep the best genes?"
1. Knockdown side, Arm 2, genes >= 10% NTC detection, own-gene entries NA (HP1 = CBX1/CBX3/CBX5, ZUFSP = ZUP1), panel naming. Three matrices from kept units: half1 ("TF|half1"), half2 ("TF|half2"), all ("TF|all"/constructs). For dual-construct TFs (no halves) construct1 serves as half1 and construct2 as half2 (stated in every table). Readings: z double-centred within each aligned matrix (gene means over TFs removed, then TF means) as primary; raw z second.
2. Confident pairs selected on Perturb-seq alone: rank all (gene, TF) entries by |z| in half1, keep the top K (K = 100, 500, 2,000, 10,000); confirmed = also in the top 5% of |z| of that TF in half2 (chance about 5%; survival rate reported). Also select-half2/confirm-half1, and the union. Per-gene version: genes whose top-|z| TF is the same in half1 and half2.
3. Binding side, rank-based, genes = the m9 universe (ABC genes in the matrix): per TF, percentile of every gene by (a) peak score P (lab q <= 0.05 peaks, signalValue x ABC; m11), (b) raw promoter reads TSS +/- 500 bp (m10), (c) promoter-only peak score (m11), (d) imputed promoter signal (m10). Per gene, the rank of each TF's percentile among the TFs.
4. Statistic per K and selection: mean binding percentile of the pair's TF at the pair's gene, and the fraction where that TF is among the gene's top 5 / top 10 TFs by binding percentile. Nulls: (i) TF labels permuted among TFs at the same genes (2,000 draws); (ii) the same TF at random genes matched on promoter-ATAC decile x NTC-expression decile (2,000 draws); (iii) the swapped TF (m7_step2 mis0, same depth tertile) at the same gene. 95% interval by bootstrap over genes (pairs resampled in gene clusters).
5. Composition beside every number: distinct TFs and genes, pairs per TF; the statistic without NRF1, without the top 3 contributing TFs, and restricted to TFs with knockdown ceiling >= 0.2. Signed: fraction of confirmed pairs with z < 0 (gene down on knockdown), by TF, descriptive.
6. Outputs: confirmed-pair table (gene, TF, z half1/half2/all, binding percentiles a-d, linked elements with a peak, promoter peak yes/no, own motif at promoter yes/no from the corrected m10 promoter scan), reverse lookup for the genes with the strongest responses, one plot (statistic vs K with null band); copies to /Users/dyy/Desktop/HEK293T-ATGTAG/perturbseq_matrix/gene_to_tf/ with README.txt.
