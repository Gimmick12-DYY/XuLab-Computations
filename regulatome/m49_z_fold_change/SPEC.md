# m49: what the Stage 1 |z| cutoffs mean as fold change (spec, 2026-11-20, written before any number)
User: "How did you calculate that |z|?" and "if you convert it to fold-change, what will be |z| >= 2 equal to?"
z (from code, m23 kd_response_m23.py resp() and m23_eval.py load()): per knockdown unit, the top 25% of its own cells by GEM-percentile sgRNA UMI; d = mean log1p(CP10k) over those cells minus the NTC reference (all NTC cells of the same GEMs, GEM means weighted by the unit's cells per GEM); z = d / sqrt(sum_g [n_g/n^2 + n_g^2/(n^2 N_g)] V_g + 1e-6), V_g = within-GEM NTC variance. Raw z (not double-centred) is what Stage 1 reads; dual-construct TFs = mean of the construct z; HP1 = mean over CBX1/3/5; own gene removed; genes with NTC detection >= 10% in the 11,228-gene universe.
Fold change, same cells and same GEM-weighted NTC reference, re-streamed from the m23 inputs and m23 cell_selection.tsv.gz (no reselection): (a) log2FC_a = d / ln 2 (the log1p CP10k difference the z is built on); (b) log2FC_b = log2((mean CP10k KD + 0.1) / (GEM-weighted mean CP10k NTC + 0.1)). Per TF, aggregated as the z (mean over constructs / CBX units). Check: recomputed z and d equal the stored m23 values.
Report over the 63 Stage 1 TFs x genes (Stage 1 OK mask): |log2FC| median and IQR (and as % change, 2^x - 1 for up, 1 - 2^-x for down, and for |.|: 2^|x| - 1) for |z| bands 1.9-2.1, 2.9-3.1, 3.8-4.2, 4.5-5.5 and for |z| >= 2 / 3 / 4 / 5; share down vs up; split by NTC expression decile (the matched-shuffle deciles); cells per TF in the top-25% set (median), and the same summary by tertile of cells per TF and for the 3 TFs with most and fewest cells. Descriptive only.
