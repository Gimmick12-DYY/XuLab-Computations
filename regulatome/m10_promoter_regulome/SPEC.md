Here every definition is re-scored at a fixed top N per TF, N = 500 and 1,000 (ABC ranked by its summed ABC score over bound elements). Per TF: list size, OR with a 95% interval from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of genes, and the accessibility-matched OR (promoter ATAC decile x CpG island, 200 draws, median). Pooled: median over TFs of (OR minus matched OR) with a 2,000-draw TF-bootstrap interval.
